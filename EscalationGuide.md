# Escalation Guide

## Severity Levels

### Sev 1

Critical outage affecting all users.

### Sev 2

Significant degradation affecting multiple users.

### Sev 3

Limited impact with workaround available.

### Sev 4

Minor issue or service request.

## Escalation Principles

- Escalate early
- Communicate clearly
- Track ownership
- Maintain regular updates
