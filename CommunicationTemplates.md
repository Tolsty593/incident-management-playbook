# Incident Communication Templates

Ready-to-use messages for each stage of an incident. Replace the text in [square brackets] before sending.

## Writing a Good Update

- **Use plain language.** Describe what users can and cannot do, not the technology behind it.
- **Say what you know, what you do not know yet, and when the next update will be.**
- **Keep to the schedule.** Send the update on time even if nothing has changed.
- **Do not guess.** Leave out causes and fix times until they are confirmed.
- **Do not blame.** Avoid naming people, teams or suppliers as being at fault.
- **Put the key point first.** Many people will read only the subject line and the first sentence.

## Who to Tell

| Audience | What they need | Channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affected users | What is not working, any workaround, when to expect news | Email, status page, service desk message |
| Service desk | The same, plus what to tell callers and how to log related tickets | Team channel |
| Management and service owners | Business impact, progress and any decisions needed | Email or direct call |
| Technical teams | Symptoms, timeline and what has been tried | Incident channel or bridge |

## Initial Notification

**Subject:** Service Incident Notification - [service name]

We are currently investigating an issue affecting [service name].

**What is affected:** [what users cannot do]

**Who is affected:** [teams, locations or all users]

**Started:** [time]

Technical teams are engaged and updates will follow.

**Next update:** [time]

**Incident reference:** [reference]

## Progress Update

**Subject:** Update [number] - Service Incident - [service name]

Investigation continues.

**Current status:**

- [Issue identified / Cause still being investigated]
- [Teams engaged]
- [Resolution activity underway]

**What has changed since the last update:** [progress, or "No change"]

**Workaround:** [steps, or "None available at present"]

**Next update:** [time]

**Incident reference:** [reference]

## Workaround Available

**Subject:** Workaround available - Service Incident - [service name]

A workaround is now available for the issue affecting [service name].

**What to do:**

1. [Step one]
2. [Step two]

**Limitations:** [anything that still does not work]

We are continuing to work on a full fix.

**Next update:** [time]

**Incident reference:** [reference]

## Resolution Notice

**Subject:** Resolved - Service Incident - [service name]

The issue has been resolved.

Services have been restored and monitoring continues.

**Resolved at:** [time]

**Duration:** [length of the incident]

**What you need to do:** [for example, sign out and back in, or "No action is needed"]

If you are still experiencing problems, please contact the service desk and quote [reference].

We apologise for the disruption.

## Post-Incident Summary

Send this after the root cause analysis is complete.

**Subject:** Incident Summary - [service name] - [date]

**What happened:** [plain-language summary]

**Impact:** [who was affected, and for how long]

**Cause:** [root cause, in plain language]

**What we did to fix it:** [resolution]

**What we are doing to prevent a repeat:** [key actions, with dates]

**Incident reference:** [reference]
