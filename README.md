# Major Incident Process

## Objective

Restore service as quickly as possible while maintaining clear stakeholder communication.

## Phase 1 - Detection

- Identify issue
- Establish impact
- Determine affected users

## Phase 2 - Assessment

- Assign severity level
- Assess business risk
- Create incident record

## Phase 3 - Escalation

- Notify technical teams
- Assign owners
- Begin investigation

## Phase 4 - Communication

- Notify stakeholders
- Provide regular updates
- Record progress

## Phase 5 - Resolution

- Implement fix
- Validate service restoration
- Close incident

## Phase 6 - Review

- Conduct retrospective
- Identify lessons learned
- Define preventative actions

# Escalation Guide

## Severity Levels

### Sev 1

Critical outage affecting all users.

### Sev 2

Significant degradation affecting multiple users.

### Sev 3

Limited impact with workaround available.

### Sev 4

Minor issue or service request.

## Escalation Principles

- Escalate early
- Communicate clearly
- Track ownership
- Maintain regular updates
